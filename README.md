# Meesho Interactive Prototype

## [Open the live prototype](https://iron-sngh.github.io/Meesho_interactive/)

An interactive, judge-ready prototype for the **Meesho DICE Challenge Season 3 — Business Track**. It demonstrates how Meesho can move a shopper from **context and intent → relevant discovery → explainable trust → proof → purchase → mission expansion** without relying on an endless generic feed.

No installation is required. Open the link above in a desktop browser and interact directly with the prototype.

---

## Quick judge walkthrough

The prototype is organised into three desktop zones:

- **Left — Persona journeys:** switch between the five use cases at any time.
- **Centre — Interactive phone:** the actual mobile product experience. Scroll and click inside the phone.
- **Right — Prototype controls + current-screen rationale:** use **Show click map** to reveal interactive regions, **Restart journey** to reset the active persona, and use the screen rationale to understand what the current screen is trying to improve.

The right panel updates dynamically as the phone moves through the journey, showing the **current screen, experience signals, and target KPIs**.

## Core product ideas demonstrated

### Mission-guided discovery
The homepage prioritises **“What do you need?”** and lightweight context over an undifferentiated product feed. Region, season or category can help with cold start, while observed behaviour should increasingly drive personalisation.

### Meesho Mall and MTrusted remain distinct
The prototype intentionally does **not** collapse these into a generic trust badge:

- **Meesho Mall = brand / provenance trust**
- **MTrusted = seller + marketplace-quality trust**

### Proof-first PDP
The product page makes a listing judgeable before asking the shopper to trust it. It includes:

- category-specific product information
- selected-variant pricing
- seller credibility
- review intelligence
- verified buyer photos / UGC
- delivery estimate
- return / replacement visibility
- payment and refund certainty

### Review intelligence
Instead of forcing users to read dozens of reviews, the prototype summarises recurring buyer themes while keeping the underlying verified reviews and visual proof accessible.

### Seller credibility
Seller trust is surfaced through understandable evidence such as seller × category performance, reliable fulfilment, lower issue incidence and listing completeness.

### Growth after product confidence
Post-confidence modules are integrated into the **same scrollable PDP**, so the shopper does not leave the product context:

1. Share & Save
2. Group Created
3. Usually Bought Together
4. Complete Your Look / Complete Your Routine
5. Suggested Products

**Suggested Products is always the final section** of the PDP.

## Interaction notes

Most visible modules are interactive. Useful paths to try include:

**Homepage → Category → Product → Product Details / Seller / Reviews / UGC → Transaction Certainty → Share & Save → Cart → Checkout → Order Confirmation**

You can also explore:

- Meesho Mall explainer
- MTrusted explainer
- seller profile
- verified review gallery
- category browsing
- wishlist
- My Orders
- account
- Usually Bought Together bundles
- Complete Your Look / Routine
- Share & Save group creation

The phone’s **Home / Categories / My Orders / Account** navigation remains fixed while the phone content scrolls.

## Prototype scope

This is a strategic product prototype, not a production Meesho build. Product prices, seller metrics, recommendations and regional merchandising are **illustrative** and exist to demonstrate the proposed interaction and trust architecture.

The prototype uses generated / fictional commerce assets where appropriate so it does not imply real merchant performance or unsupported marketplace statistics.

---

## What judges should look for

The central question is not simply whether the UI looks different. The prototype is designed to show how Meesho can:

**make discovery more relevant → make trust more explainable → make evaluation easier → reduce first-order hesitation → extend confidence into the next mission.**

The same trust and proof system remains available across regional, festive, branded, marketplace-quality and college-style journeys.
